How It Works

Find or return lost belongings on campus in a few simple steps.

🔐

Step 1: Sign Up or Login

Create an account or log in to access the SAU Lost and Found website.

📝

Step 2: Report an Item

Select Lost or Found, enter the item details, location and upload an image if available.

🔍

Step 3: Search for Items

Browse the reported items and check whether someone has found your missing belongings.

📞

Step 4: Contact the Owner

Use the available contact information to coordinate with the person who reported the item.

🤝

Step 5: Get Your Item Back

Verify ownership and arrange a safe meeting to return the lost item.
